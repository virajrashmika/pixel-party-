MINI RACING

Two players, one keyboard, split screen. Best of 3 races: City Night, Desert Sunset, Mountain Forest.

BACK TO MENU (ESC)

PLAYER 1 · LEFT SCREEN

W speed up S brake AD steer G drift (hold while steering at speed) F use item / ready

PLAYER 2 · RIGHT SCREEN

↑ speed up ↓ brake ←→ steer LNum . drift (hold while steering at speed) KNum 0 use item / ready

HOW TO PLAY

Both press their item key to start. After 3, 2, 1, GO! race 3000 m to the finish line. Win 2 races to win the match. Each race is a new track: City Night, Desert Sunset, Mountain Forest.

THE TRACK

Bends push you outward, and the grass slows you down and throws up dust. Uphill slows you a little, downhill speeds you up. Fly over a big hill top at high speed and you jump: no steering in the air!

DRIFTING

Going fast? Hold your drift key while steering: the car slides sideways. Steer into the slide for a tighter turn, the other way to straighten out. The longer you drift, the sparks go blue, orange, purple: let go for a small, medium or big boost.

ITEMS

Drive through a ? box to get an item, then press your item key. Nitro = speed. Bomb and oil drop behind you. Rocket fires straight ahead. Homing rocket chases your rival. Shield blocks one hit. Shockwave blasts a nearby rival away. The player behind gets the better items.
