BOSS FIGHT

Co-op. Each player breaks one leg of the Stone King, then you both smash its core.

HOW TO WIN

The boss blocks the middle, so each player can only reach their own leg. Break a leg and it stays broken for a few seconds. Break both legs at the same time and the boss collapses: its glowing core drops down. Smash it together. Too slow and the broken leg heals halfway.

DEFENCE

Block (hold) stops the damage but pushes you back. Parry (tap block just before a hit) takes no damage and gives you double damage for 3 seconds. Parrying an arm swipe also stuns the boss. Dodge roll dodges anything, even the laser.

BOSS ATTACKS

The floor glows red where an attack is about to land. Arm swipe and stomp shockwave: block, parry or roll. Eye laser (red aim lines): can't be blocked, so move or roll. Below half health it gets angry: falling rocks and a double stomp. A knocked-down player gets back up after 10 seconds while the other is still standing.
