REFLEX DUEL

Two players, one keyboard. First to 3 catches wins.

BACK TO MENU (ESC)

PLAYER 1 · LEFT HAND

F catch

PLAYER 2 · RIGHT HAND

K or Num 0 catch

HOW TO PLAY

Both players press their key to get ready. Each ruler hangs from a clip. After a random wait, both rulers drop at the same moment. Catch yours first to win the point. Press during the wait and your opponent gets the point.

THE NUMBERS ARE REAL

A dropped ruler falls d = ½·g·t², the same as the school ruler test. 200 ms means 19.6 cm, 250 ms means 30.7 cm, and after 319 ms the 50 cm ruler slips right through your fingers.

KEYBOARD TIP

If one player's key stops working while the other holds theirs, the keyboard is "ghosting". Player 2 can switch from K to Num 0.
