PIXEL PONG

Two players, one keyboard. First to 5 points wins.

BACK TO MENU (ESC)

PLAYER 1 · LEFT HAND

W up S down

PLAYER 2 · RIGHT HAND

↑ up ↓ down

HOW TO PLAY

Both players press one of their keys to get ready. Move your paddle to hit the ball back. Miss it and your rival scores. Every hit makes the ball a little faster.

AIM WITH YOUR PADDLE

Where the ball hits your paddle decides where it goes. The middle sends it straight back, the edges send it off at a steep angle: a power hit.

KEYBOARD TIP

If one player's keys stop working while the other holds theirs, the keyboard is "ghosting". Player 2 can switch from the arrows to Num 8 and Num 5.
